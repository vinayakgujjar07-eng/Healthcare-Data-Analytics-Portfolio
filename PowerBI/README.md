\# Healthcare Analytics Dashboard



\## Project Overview

An interactive healthcare analytics dashboard developed using Microsoft Power BI to analyse hospital admissions, patient demographics, billing patterns, insurance providers and hospital operational performance.



\## Dataset

\- Total records analysed: 54,966

\- Medical conditions: 6

\- Insurance providers: 5

\- Admission categories: 3

\- Dataset period: May 2019 – May 2024



\## Key Performance Indicators

\- Total Admissions

\- Total Revenue

\- Average Billing

\- Average Patient Age

\- Average Length of Stay

\- Total Hospitals



\## Dashboard Features

\- Interactive KPI cards

\- Admissions by medical condition

\- Revenue by medical condition

\- Patient age-group analysis

\- Gender distribution

\- Insurance provider analysis

\- Admission-type analysis

\- Monthly admission trends

\- Average length of stay

\- Interactive slicers

\- Reset filters functionality



\## Tools and Technologies

\- Microsoft Power BI

\- DAX

\- Power Query

\- Microsoft Excel / CSV

\- Data Visualisation



\## Key Business Insights

\- Patients above 65 years account for the largest admission volume.

\- Diabetes contributes the highest total revenue among medical conditions.

\- Cigna records the highest admission volume among insurance providers.

\- Elective admissions have the highest admission volume.

\- Male and female admissions are almost equally distributed.

`Healthcare\_Analytics\_Dashboard.pbix`



\## Limitations

The dataset appears synthetic. The findings are analytical observations from the supplied dataset and should not be interpreted as actual hospital performance or real-world healthcare trends.
